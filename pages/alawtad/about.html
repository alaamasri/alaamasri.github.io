About us

From the heart of Amman, Alawtad for Food Supplies first opened its doors in 2005, specializing in flavorings for the food and beverage industry.

Our story

From the outset, the company has specialized in the creation, development, production and distribution of flavorings for the food and beverage industry.

Working across food industries and various countries, our company is uniquely positioned to meet some of the region's main needs. We have brought world-class technology and developments to the regional marketplace through innovative products, solutions and services.

Our quality gauge

Al-Awtad for Food Supplies expertise is defined by:

  • In-depth knowledge of current markets

  • Recognized expertise in the sourcing of raw materials

  • An experienced R&D team

  • High-performance research and manufacturing tools

These key elements allow us to develop innovative flavorings in a cost-effective manner, tailored to the aromatic profiles required by our clients and in accordance with relevant legislation.

Our products

As an innovator in beverage enrichment, we provide a full selection of micro-nutrients, functional ingredients and carbonated colors for the beverage segment.

These ingredients can be supplied individually, or combined in the form of a user-friendly, customer-specific Quality-Blend.

We offer extensive expertise in the nutritional fortification of beverages. Our market research helps you understand consumer health needs, while our personalized scientific, technical and production support enables you to design and develop innovative functional beverages with optimal flavor, stability and consistency.

In the complex world of beverage fortification, only the strongest partnerships deliver a sustainable competitive edge.
